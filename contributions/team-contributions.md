## 3. Academic Integrity & Reading Status Statement

### No Conflation of AI Ingestion with Personal Reading
- The student team explicitly states that automated bibliographic ingestion and AI-assisted cross-checking do **not** constitute personal cover-to-cover reading by individual students.
- Publisher verification checks, abstract readings, and personal member readings are clearly separated in Table XI and Table XII of the survey annexure and in the master literature matrix.

### Progressive Five-Tier Evaluation Framework
The team enforces a strict 5-tier evaluation framework across all project records:
1. **Candidate Registration**: Registering candidate works into the literature matrix (32 unique candidate studies).
2. **Source Verification**: Confirming DOI existence, publication details, and auditing claims against accessible publisher pages and preprints.
3. **PDF Availability**: Downloading legally permitted open-access PDFs into `research-papers/original_pdfs/` without paywall circumvention.
4. **Personal Member Reading**: Detailed human cover-to-cover reading by students (ongoing/in progress).
5. **Inclusion in Narrative Synthesis**: Synthesizing 26 core studies into Section IV and Section V tables and discussion.
